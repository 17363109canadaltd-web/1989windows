Who we are

1989 Windows and Doors supplies aluminium building envelope systems for commercial and residential projects. Our range covers unitised and stick curtain walls from 35mm to 200mm series, the Eiffel collection of French windows and doors, and a 2025 line of system entry doors. Every system uses thermal breaks, visible drainage and premium hardware, and our team provides overseas installation and technical support.

Full-frame aluminium glass curtain wall

A modern, lightweight façade system widely used on high-rise and commercial buildings.

  • Resists rust and UV degradation
  • Lighter structural load than masonry
  • Sleek, minimal appearance
  • Large glass areas with slim frames

Hidden-frame aluminium glass curtain wall

The structural aluminium frame is concealed for a seamless, all-glass look, ideal where transparency comes first.

  • Luxury hotels and resorts
  • Corporate offices and headquarters
  • High-end retail
  • Airports and museums

80–200 × 65mm

Half-hidden-frame aluminium glass curtain wall

A hybrid of full-frame and hidden-frame designs: some mullions are concealed and some framing stays visible, balancing appearance, strength and cost.

100–200 × 65mm

Thermal-break full-frame curtain wall

Full-frame structure with added thermal insulation, for energy-efficient buildings in extreme climates.

  • Reduced heat loss and gain
  • Helps prevent condensation and mould
  • Lower heating and cooling costs
  • Meets modern energy codes

Minimalism hidden-frame curtain wall

Minimalism thermal-break full-frame curtain wall

Door leaf structural system

  2. 6060-T66 aluminium profilesExtra-hard European-standard aluminium that stays stable and resists deformation for long, safe use.
